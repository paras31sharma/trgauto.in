How the catalogue works.

Model names and market coverage are cross-checked against current new-car listings. Detailed variant rows are only shown where TRG Auto has a verified source. City on-road figures are estimates and should never be treated as dealer quotes.

Sources used for market coverage:
CarWale — current new-car catalogue and brand pages (September 2026).
CarDekho — current new-car catalogue and brand pages (September 2026).
Manufacturer pricing/configurator pages for detailed variants where available.
